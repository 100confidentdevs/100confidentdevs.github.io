About

100 Confident Devs is a community where I personally teach 100 developers how to master Roblox Studio — and other game development pursuits.

The goal is simple: take developers from unsure to confident. Confident enough to ship real games, solve their own problems, and — most importantly — confident enough to turn around and teach the next person. Every dev I train becomes someone who can train others.

We learn together through hands-on seminars, shared projects, and a tight Discord community where questions get answered and progress gets celebrated. Joining is free — jump into the Discord above, introduce yourself, and start learning.
